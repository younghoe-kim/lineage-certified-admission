## Scope notes

Evidence of CL-PBX: outputs/verdicts/path_b_certificates.csv (all certificates), outputs/verdicts/path_b_open_witnesses.csv (all 659 open fibers), outputs/verdicts/path_b_open_witnesses_public.csv (the subset that make check rechecks from the outputs alone) and outputs/verdicts/path_b_open_summary.json (counts and hashes). The independent verifier rechecks every open fiber from the input view (outputs/checks/independent_checks.csv).

Scope of CL-KG. It concerns WN18 and WN18RR only. A split withholds each held-out triple by its identity and admits the rest of the graph, the analogue of identity-based admission for splits. WN18 merges synsets that share an offset across parts of speech, so each certified pair is matched with one part of speech per synset. The WN18RR test count equals the one published by Akrami et al. (SIGMOD 2020). Reading the Domain row in both directions adds 154 WN18 test triples (4,658 in all). Held-out triples without a training witness are UNVERIFIED. Scope of CL-SEN12MS. The view is the merged counts that the official loader computes for the 10-class label, and the protected query is the released 17-class label; counts, map and labels come from the third-party release. With one exposed image of a total function, Theorem 1(a) applies and the certified patches have a closed form. The rival states of UNVERIFIED fibers are states of the fiber, not observed patches.
